### C. AUTOS DEALER — BLOCKED_EXISTING_CONTRACT (no Quick intake built)

The canonical Dealer product has no dealership-profile-only publication: the dealer's main row IS a vehicle listing (`inventory_role = main`, `AUTOS_NEGOCIOS_ADAPTER.publicRoute = /clasificados/autos/vehiculo/{id}`), `getAutosPreviewCompletenessIssues("negocios")` requires a vehicle title (year + make + model), a price / monthly estimate and media, the application is vehicle-first (`AutosNegociosVehicleApplicationSteps`), and the API derives the row title from the vehicle (`autosClassifiedsListingService.ts:426-427`). A profile-only Quick would therefore have to fabricate a vehicle or reuse dealership photos as a vehicle — both forbidden. Per Gate 7 the Dealer category is STOPPED for Quick; the Quick chooser and the staff launchpad link Dealer directly to the existing application (`/publicar/autos/negocios`) with honest copy. Exact dependency: **a dealership can only exist on Leonix as at least one real vehicle listing.**

### D. BIENES RAÍCES NEGOCIO / AGENT — BLOCKED_EXISTING_CONTRACT (no Quick intake built)

The canonical agent product has no agent-profile-only publication: the agente-individual application maps into the negocio publish shape with `titulo`, `precio`, `photoUrls` and `publicationType` derived from the property (`mapAgenteResidencialFormStateToNegocioForPublish.ts:243-301`), `gateBienesRaicesNegocioPreview` requires a titled, priced, photographed property with `petsAllowed`, and the public route is a property page (`/clasificados/anuncio/{id}`). A profile-only Quick would have to fabricate a property. Per Gate 8 the category is STOPPED for Quick; the chooser and launchpad link directly to the existing selector (`/publicar/bienes-raices`). FSBO (`br_fsbo_45d`, Classifieds) is untouched. Exact dependency: **an agent/business can only exist on Leonix as at least one real property listing.**
